Beyond the Throw
QB Film Breakdown Checklist
A Pre-Session Framework for Coaches & Quarterbacks

Before You Press Play

  • Set a specific objective for this session (mechanics, protection ID, decision-making, red zone)
  • Pull only the relevant clips — do not watch full game without a purpose filter
  • Have your note sheet ready (position, formation, coverage, outcome columns)
  • Eliminate distractions — phones away, focused time block (30–45 min max per session)

Pre-Snap Read (Every Rep)

  • Identify formation and personnel package
  • Identify defensive front alignment (3-4, 4-3, odd/even)
  • Identify coverage shell at the snap (Cover 1, Cover 2, Cover 3, Cover 4, Man)
  • Did QB's eyes get to the right key (safety depth, LB alignment, slot corner leverage)?
  • Did QB communicate protection / alert the line before snap?

Footwork & Mechanics

  • Stance at center — balanced, weight slightly forward, no false step
  • First step consistent with play design (quick game 3-step, drop game 5/7-step)
  • Hips loaded at the top of drop — not early, not late
  • Weight transfer into throw — push off back foot, drive through front hip
  • Elbow above shoulder at release — no sidearm creep under pressure
  • Follow through — thumb down, palm out, no short-arming

Decision Making & Eyes

  • Did QB work through the progression or lock onto Route 1?
  • Did QB's eyes move defenders before delivering the ball?
  • Was the window identified pre-snap matched post-snap?
  • Did QB know where to go with the ball against pressure (hot route, check-down)?
  • On interceptions / near-picks: what was the trigger? (coverage, coverage, pressure, or poor mechanics?)

Pocket Presence & Extension

  • Did QB feel pressure and step up into the pocket vs. drift backward?
  • Did QB maintain eyes downfield while moving (no eyes dropping to rush)?
  • Was there a sack or near-sack that resulted from late processing vs. poor protection?
  • When scrambling: eyes still scanning for downfield options before pulling it down?

Post-Session Debrief

  • Identify 1–2 repeating errors (mechanical or mental) — write them down
  • Identify 1 play to use as a teaching rep (good example)
  • Assign a drill prescription that directly addresses the error
  • Set the focus point for next individual period on the practice field

Post-session notes should be in the QB's hands within 24 hours of the session for maximum retention.
