## FAQ'S

What/How should I create my original highlight footage?

Most college coaches review highlights from Hudl, Qwikcut, and sometimes on YouTube or even basic phone edits. We can edit and tailor any existing highlight on Hudl or Qwikcut, or build a brand-new one from your raw clips/game footage. Just upload what you have. We handle the rest.

How does this stand out from other recruiting services?

As a former Division 1 coach for 4 years, I sat in recruiting meetings every cycle. Not once did we or our rivals sign a player from a 3rd-party recruiting service.

We built our own boards using highlight films we evaluated ourselves. We focused first on film quality and size, then only trusted referrals from proven coaches/trainers. Our service delivers what actually works: pro-level films from real recruiting insiders.

How long does the process take?

Typically 7 to 14 days from review to final delivery, depending on footage volume and revisions. We prioritize quick turnaround so you can start sending to coaches fast.

What if I don't have much game film?

We review your raw clips first. If there's enough usable material to create a strong showcase, we'll proceed. If not, we'll honestly tell you and issue a full refund. No risk.

Why is a professional edit so important?

Coaches watch hundreds of highlight films quickly, often in long recruiting sessions lasting hours. After about 30 minutes, they all start to blend together. Poor editing, slow starts, or disorganization causes an instant "No" or "recheck later" — where your film gets buried and forgotten. A polished, strategic film grabs their attention in seconds and keeps you on the board.

How do you make the film "coach-ready"?

We front-load your best plays, use clean cuts, add clear player ID (arrows/numbers), and sequence strategically to show all your strengths quickly. No music, slow-mo, or distractions. Just what coaches want to see in recruiting rooms.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

Yes. Cancel anytime before we deliver the final film for a 100% refund. We also refund fully if, after review, we can't create a quality film that will help you stand out.
